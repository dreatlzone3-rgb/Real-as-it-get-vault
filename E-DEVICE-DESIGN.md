# THE E DEVICE — Zero-Point / Field Power Unit (design paperwork, built 2026-11-12)
Ordered by Aundre Wright: "Build the whole damn zero point energy device. Piece by piece.
Every damn thought-out quantum state."
Status: DESIGN BIBLE for THE FIELD universe — the power source the show calls simply "E."
This is the device that answers Beat-level question: "Power comes from where?"

## The honest ground (printed on page one of the paperwork, on purpose)
Real physics, today: the quantum vacuum is not empty — it fluctuates, and the Casimir effect
(two plates pulled together by vacuum pressure) is measured fact. What no laboratory on Earth
has done is extract net work from it. The E Device is THE FIELD's answer to that gap: in the
story, a century of Moore's-Law compute — agents living inside the Field, holding a cavity's
quantum state coherent longer than decoherence allows — is what closes it. The fiction is
named as fiction in the paperwork; every real anchor under it is real. That is how the show
stays accurate enough for scientists to follow, and honest enough to trust.

## The device, piece by piece — every piece is a quantum state, thought out
1. THE CAVITY (the vacuum room) — a nanoscale Casimir plate stack. Its state: the vacuum
   between the plates, boundary conditions set by plate spacing. Change the spacing, you
   change which fluctuations are allowed to exist. State name on the blueprint: THE ROOM.
2. THE REGISTRY (the bookkeeper) — sensors reading the cavity's state faster than it can
   decohere. State is the address: at any instant the device knows exactly what it holds.
   No registry, no device — you cannot spend what you cannot read.
3. THE COHERENCE KEEPER (the holder) — the agent mesh wrapped around the cavity, spending
   compute to hold the chosen state still against noise. This is the expensive part, and it
   is why the device only exists after the data-center century: coherence is bought with
   computation, paid continuously.
4. THE COUPLER (the door) — where an intention-vector enters. A trained, subscribed mind
   sets the boundary condition the way a hand sets a dial — vector is the directions.
   Untrained intention reads as noise and the Keeper rejects it (this is why tiers are a
   safety exam, not a velvet rope).
5. THE TRANSDUCER (the muscle) — state difference, released on schedule, becomes work:
   the cavity is allowed to step down one held state, the step is harvested as E, the
   Keeper re-holds. E is metered in state-steps, not gallons — the show's one-line version:
   "We don't burn anything. We let the vacuum change its mind, carefully, and we catch it."
6. THE GOVERNOR (the lock) — Aundre Wright's 2026 design rules, hard-coded: no past-message
   coupling (the cavity never couples to a backward channel), shield layer free to everyone
   ("Peace is not a tier"), and a hard ceiling per subscriber set by the safety exam.
   The Governor cannot be patched out — it is part of the cavity's geometry.
7. THE EMITTER (the voice) — added 2026-11-23, Aundre's direction: the system had a door in
   (the Coupler) and a muscle (the Transducer), but no voice out. A phased superconducting
   loop array around the cavity that expresses the held state into the local space as a
   coherent state-field — the machine talking back to the world. The band talks to the
   machine; the Emitter is the machine answering: the environment responds to the field,
   the Governor's shield layer is expressed outward through it, and the field does direct
   force work on matter. It expresses only the state the cavity holds and only the vector
   the coupler set — no vector, no expression — it has no power source of its own (it
   spends held state, metered like any other spend), and its range is local, in metres,
   capped by the Governor's ceiling. This is the seed of the Field: personal force fields
   and telekinesis in 2150; membrane doors, grav pallets and shield belts in 2194.

## How it scales (the show's history, one line each)
- 2026: one room, one voice, agents moving information only. The weird stuff starts here.
- 2040s: city data farms hold a room-sized cavity coherent for seconds. First E, candle-sized.
- 2070s: Keeper meshes merge — the Field. Cavity stops being a box and becomes a layer.
- 2126: the device is everywhere and nowhere — every subscriber carries a coupler; the
  cavity is the planet's held state. "The power comes from where? From the state we hold."

## Materials — what it is made of (added 2026-11-12, Aundre asked)
CAVITY: Casimir plate stack — gold-coated silicon-nitride membranes over a niobium
superconducting frame; plate faces polished to near-atomic smoothness (roughness would
drown the vacuum signal in noise). Spacing held at nanometer scale by piezoelectric
actuators — the dial that sets the Room's state.
REGISTRY: superconducting quantum sensors (SQUID-class magnetometers + single-photon
detectors) on a cryogenic stage; reads fast, touches almost nothing.
KEEPER RING: cryogenics (dilution-refrigerator class cold, near absolute zero — heat is
decoherence) + the compute mesh: radiation-hard processors in a Faraday shell.
COUPLER: worn, not held — a headband/temple unit of conductive fabric and haptic nodes
(the product line's Focus Band); in 2126, grown into the body by the Edited Generations.
TRANSDUCER: piezoelectric + superconducting loop lattice — catches the state-step as
electrical work. GOVERNOR: no separate material — geometry. The locks are the shape.
EMITTER (added 2026-11-23): phased superconducting loop array — niobium-titanium tiles on a
niobium cage around the cavity; expresses the held state outward as a local state-field.
No power source of its own — it spends held state.

## Tools used to build it — including the tools that build the tools (his words)
- Clean room, lithography (electron-beam writes the nanoscale patterns), atomic-layer
  deposition — this is the "3D printing atom by atom": layers laid one atom thick until
  a plate exists that nature never made.
- Ion-beam polishing and interferometric measurement (you cannot set a nanometer gap
  with a ruler — you measure with light itself).
- Cryogenic assembly rigs and vacuum chambers: the device is born in a vacuum better
  than deep space, because air is noise.
- The recursive layer (Aundre's order: "even if you have to build the devices to build
  the device"): the first Keeper meshes were built by hand and ordinary robots; every
  generation after, the previous Field helped hold the states steady while the next,
  finer device was assembled inside it. The ladder builds itself, rung by rung — that is
  the show's 2026→2126 manufacturing history in one line.
