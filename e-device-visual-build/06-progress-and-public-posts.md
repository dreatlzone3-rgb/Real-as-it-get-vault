## What this is

The E Device is a zero-point energy device — a machine built around the quantum vacuum. Empty space is not empty: quantum fields fluctuate even in their ground state, and the Casimir effect is the measured proof that changing the space changes the vacuum. The E Device holds a controlled vacuum state inside a 24-layer Casimir plate stack and steps that state, carefully, harvesting the difference as usable energy — E. Nothing is burned. Nothing is consumed.

Honest ground: the Casimir effect and vacuum fluctuation are real, measured physics (Casimir predicted 1948; Lamoreaux measured 1997). Net-work extraction from the vacuum is this design's stated leap, presented openly as the blueprint of the machine that would do it.

Full design: see `E-DEVICE-DESIGN.md` and `e-device-visual-build/` in this repository.
