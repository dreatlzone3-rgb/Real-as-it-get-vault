## Standing locks for every sheet in this file

- **The E Device is a SYSTEM, never a glowing box held in the hand.** Hand-held and worn objects are couplers and keys only — never the source.
- **Look:** grounded photoreal tech, engineering-drawing / 3D-model tech-sheet style. Real materials, real fasteners, real cable runs. No cartoon, no generic glow-box, no fantasy ornament.
- **2194 register:** scientist/philosopher terms only — state, boundary condition, coherence, registry, state-step. No frequency / hum / pulse framing anywhere on any sheet.
- **Honest ground, printed on Sheet 00 and repeated in the footer of every sheet:** "Casimir effect / vacuum fluctuation: real, measured physics. Net-work extraction from the vacuum: this story's fictional leap."
- **Sheet format (all sheets):** 16:9 landscape tech sheet, off-white drafting background (#E8E6E0), thin graphite rule lines, front / side / top orthographic views at consistent scale, one shaded perspective render bottom-right, dimension lines in millimetres, callout numbers in circles keyed to the callout list printed on the sheet, title block bottom-left reading: piece name, state name, scale, sheet number, and the origin credit line above.
- **Global palette (all sheets):** niobium frame — matte slate grey #5A6068, brushed; gold membrane — muted engineering gold #B9974F, satin, never mirror-polished in renders; silicon-nitride — warm ceramic ivory #D8D2C4; cryogenic hardware — frost white #EEF1F2 with machined aluminium #AEB4B8; Faraday shell — dark graphite #2E3236, fine mesh texture; accent for callouts and dimension lines ONLY — signal red #B03A2E (ink, never a glow); human skin / hand reference — natural tones, photoreal.

## SHEET 01 — PIECE 1: THE CAVITY / THE ROOM

**What it is:** A nanoscale Casimir plate stack. Gold-coated silicon-nitride membranes stretched over a niobium superconducting frame. Plate spacing held at nanometer scale by piezoelectric actuators — the spacing IS the state: change the spacing, you change which vacuum fluctuations are allowed to exist between the plates.

**Views:**
- **Front:** square stack face-on — concentric membrane frames, actuator housings at the four corners, frame bolt pattern visible.
- **Side:** the money view — layer diagram in true proportion with a magnified inset: membrane, vacuum gap (labelled in nanometres), membrane, repeated 24 layers; piezoelectric actuator column drawn at full height beside the stack.
- **Top:** frame plan — membrane tensioning ring, actuator wiring channels routed to one edge only (the service edge).

**Scale:** stack 380 mm × 380 mm × 210 mm — roughly the size of a large book standing on a lab bench; drawn beside a photoreal human hand (fingers clear of the membrane face, on the frame edge only — nobody touches the Room). One membrane alone: 0.5 mm thick, shown in inset against a human hair for thickness reference.

**Materials / finish:** membranes — muted satin gold over warm ivory silicon-nitride, plate faces polished to near-atomic smoothness (drawn matte-flat; a mirror shine would read as decoration, the bible says roughness drowns the signal — the finish is precision, not jewellery); frame — matte slate niobium; actuators — machined aluminium housings, ceramic tips.

**Callouts on sheet:** 1 Gold-coated silicon-nitride membrane · 2 Vacuum gap — spacing sets the state · 3 Niobium superconducting frame · 4 Piezoelectric actuator (×4, nanometer positioning) · 5 Membrane tensioning ring · 6 Service edge — all connections exit here · 7 State name: THE ROOM.

## SHEET 03 — PIECE 3: THE COHERENCE KEEPER

This is the expensive part, and the reason the device only exists after the data-center century.

**Views:**

## SHEET 05 — PIECE 5: THE TRANSDUCER

**What it is:** The muscle. A piezoelectric + superconducting loop lattice forming the base of the cold core. On schedule, the Cavity is allowed to step down one held state; the Transducer catches that state-step and converts it to electrical work. Then the Keeper re-holds. E is metered in state-steps, not gallons.

**Views:**
- **Front:** lattice base face-on — repeating loop cells in a rigid frame, bus bars exiting the service edge.
- **Side:** stack order section — Transducer lattice at the bottom, Cavity ghosted above it, a single state-step drawn as a stepped line diagram (ink steps, descending one step, labelled "one state-step = one unit of E").
- **Top:** lattice plan — cell grid 8 × 8, service edge, lifting points marked (this base is the heaviest single piece).

**Scale:** lattice plinth 900 mm × 900 mm × 160 mm, mass callout 210 kg. Human hand on the frame edge; a second inset shows one loop cell (90 mm) beside a coin for scale.

**Materials / finish:** loop cells — niobium alloy, matte slate; piezoelectric elements — pale ceramic blocks, warm ivory, matching the silicon-nitride tone for family resemblance; bus bars — solid copper, brushed, unlacquered.

## SHEET 06 — PIECE 6: THE GOVERNOR

**What it is:** The lock. **No separate material — the Governor is geometry.** Aundre Wright's 2026 design rules, hard-coded into the shape of the Cavity's boundary itself, so they cannot be patched out: (1) no past-message coupling — the cavity never couples to a backward channel; (2) the shield layer is free to everyone — "Peace is not a tier"; (3) a hard ceiling per subscriber, set by the safety exam.

**Views:** (this sheet is a geometry sheet — no object render; the perspective corner carries a small render of the Cavity with the boundary lines overlaid in red ink)
- **Front:** Cavity outline with the boundary-condition geometry drawn full — the asymmetric plate-frame profile that physically prevents backward coupling, dimensioned like any other part.
- **Side:** coupling-path diagram — forward path drawn solid and permitted; backward path drawn, then physically interrupted by the frame geometry (a drawn gap in the metal, labelled "no material, no path").
- **Top:** access diagram — shield layer drawn as the outermost ring, marked FREE / NO SUBSCRIPTION; subscriber ceiling drawn as a hard mechanical stop on the boundary-condition range, dimensioned, not a software setting.

**Scale:** drawn at Cavity scale (380 mm reference outline) with the same hand at the frame edge for continuity; the access diagram is not to scale and says so on the sheet.

**Materials / finish:** none — and the sheet says so in the title block: "Material: none. The locks are the shape." All other finishes ghosted pale so the red-ink geometry carries the sheet.

**Callouts on sheet:** 1 Asymmetric boundary geometry — backward coupling has no path · 2 Interrupted coupling route (drawn gap in metal) · 3 Shield layer — free to everyone · 4 Hard ceiling — mechanical stop, set by safety exam · 5 State name: THE GOVERNOR — "Peace is not a tier."

## SHEET 08 — PIECE 7: THE EMITTER

**Added 2026-11-23, on Aundre's direction** — he identified the gap himself: the system had an input (the Coupler — the band talks TO the machine) and a harvest (the Transducer) but no output. The Emitter is the machine talking BACK to the world. Pieces 1–6 are unchanged.

**What it is:** The voice. A phased superconducting loop array carried on a niobium cage around the cold core, inside the Keeper's Faraday shell. Where the Transducer cashes a state-step out as electricity, the Emitter expresses the held state itself into the local space around the device as a coherent state-field. Through it, the environment answers the field (lights and devices respond to the wearer's set state), the Governor's shield layer is expressed outward as a personal / area shield, and the field does direct force work on matter — the seed of the 2150 Field effects (personal force fields, telekinesis) and of 2194 emitter-side tech (Membrane Door, Grav Pallet, Shield Belt). **Hard limits, drawn on the sheet, not footnoted:** it expresses ONLY the state the Cavity currently holds and ONLY the vector the Coupler set — no vector, no expression; the band alone does nothing. Range is local — metres — capped by the Governor's per-subscriber ceiling (Sheet 06). It holds no independent power source: expression spends held state, metered by the Registry/Keeper accounting like any other spend.

**Views:**
- **Front:** emitter cage face-on — three phased loop rings stacked around the ghosted cold core at centre, loop tiles evenly spaced on each ring, phase-drive loom exiting at the service edge.
- **Side:** section through the cold core — rings around the Cavity and Registry (both ghosted, dashed outlines), the expressed-field boundary drawn as red-ink dashed lines extending outward from the rings and STOPPING at the dimensioned Governor ceiling radius — the limit is drawn, not implied.
- **Top:** plan — concentric order readable at a glance: Cavity (ghosted, centre), Registry ring (ghosted), the three Emitter rings drawn bold, phase-tile spacing dimensioned, service edge marked.

**Scale:** cage outer diameter 840 mm × 480 mm tall, carried around the 620 mm Registry ring with clearance (concentric order: Cavity 380 mm → Registry 620 mm → Emitter 840 mm, all inside the 900 mm Transducer plinth footprint and the 4200 × 3100 × 2900 mm system). Three rings of 32 loop tiles each — 96 tiles, each tile 60 mm × 60 mm. Human hand at the cage rim for continuity with Sheets 01–02. Expression-range inset (not to scale, and the sheet says so): operator figure with the expressed-field boundary at 3,000 mm radius — personal scale, room-sized at most, never more.

**Materials / finish:** loop tiles — niobium-titanium alloy, matte slate, dull like the Registry sensor cans; cage — matte slate niobium, same family as the Cavity frame; phase-drive loom — black matte fibre + coax, loomed like the Registry readout, service edge only; drive crate at the service edge — machined aluminium. The expressed field is drawn ONLY as red-ink dashed boundary lines — diagram ink, never a glow. Sheets show hardware and geometry, not operation.

**Callouts on sheet:** 1 Phased superconducting loop ring (×3) · 2 Loop / phase tile (×96) · 3 Emitter cage — niobium frame · 4 Phase-drive loom — service edge · 5 Cold core position (ghosted — Cavity + Registry) · 6 Expression boundary — radius set by Governor ceiling (see Sheet 06) · 7 State name: THE EMITTER — "The machine talking back. No vector, no expression."
