# THE E DEVICE — VISUAL BUILD, PASS 1: STORYBOARD — PART TWO: "THE DEMONSTRATION" (KEYNOTE EXPLAINER)

**Project:** THE FIELD (2194) — Part Two. Part One ("THE BUILD") was the clean room: one man building the device alone. Part Two flips the camera: AUNDRE is on a big stage, presenting the finished device to the public — a full keynote demonstration, the way the big companies launch their products, except the man on stage is the builder himself and the product is the E Device.

It is his stage, his product, his mouth; nobody else presents, demos, or shares the stage as a presenter. ## Standing rules for this sequence

- **Format:** a product-launch keynote, shot like one — dark hall, giant screen behind the presenter, live demos on stage, audience in frame. But the grammar serves him: every time a normal keynote would reach for a price, a tier, or a subscription, this one reaches for the Governor.
- **Aundre's wardrobe (locked):** his own look — red-green-blue plaid flannel, olive-green knit beanie, black rectangular glasses, silver chain. No suit. No black turtleneck. He is not dressing like them; he is presenting as himself.
- **Camera:** keynote coverage — wide hall shots, tracking shots that walk with Aundre across the stage, close-ups and inserts of the demo rigs, big-screen inserts, audience reaction cutaways. Nothing static, no locked-off shots, no slow zoom on a still.
- **Dialogue only.** Aundre presents — to the hall, to the camera, to the volunteer beside him. Audience members speak only in the Q&A panel. There is no narrator voice-over anywhere in this sequence.
- **2194 register:** scientist/philosopher terms — state, boundary condition, coherence, registry, state-step. No frequency / hum / pulse framing in dialogue, captions or sound notes.
- **Honest ground (carried from Part One, Panel 2):** the placard line appears on the giant screen, in frame: "Casimir effect: measured fact. Net work from the vacuum: our leap." The device is presented as engineering — a design, a build, a leap that is named as a leap. Never sold as magic, never sold as finished science.
- **The device is a system.** The big screen shows the system — Cavity/Room, Registry, Coherence Keeper, Coupler, Transducer, Governor-as-shape. No panel shows anyone holding a glowing box. The only object handled on stage is the Focus Band, and it is a key, never a source.
- **The shield layer is free.** This is the keynote's centre of gravity: "The shield isn't a tier. Peace is not a tier." No price is ever attached to safety, on any screen, in any line.
- **People:** Aundre alone on stage for the whole presentation, except: one audience volunteer (Panel 13), the new arrival from Part One (Panel 16, and listening in Panel 20), and the audience itself — everyday people, shown plainly.
- **The named audience (locked):** Palmer Luckey, Jason Wang, Dario Amodei, Mark Zuckerberg, and Tim Cook sit together in the audience as themselves — seated, watching, reacting. Cutaways to them land only at the big beats (the scaled-up demos, the price slide, the free shield, the closing line). They get one Q&A exchange at most (Panel 17). No stage time, no demo, no applause shot that takes the film off Aundre for more than a beat. The contrast is the point: the men who present for a living are in the seats for this one.

**Panel count: 20 panels.**
