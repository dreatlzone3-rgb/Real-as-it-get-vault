# THE E DEVICE — VISUAL BUILD, PASS 1: STORYBOARD — "THE BUILD" (REVEAL SEQUENCE)

**Project:** THE FIELD (2194) — how the E Device is made: the tools build the tools, then the device builds itself a ladder.
**Pass:** 1 — storyboard only. NO video generation in this pass, and none until Aundre approves the Pass 2 model sheets (see `01-device-model-sheets.md`) and this board.
**Source bible:** `~/workspace/your_files/the-field-intention-storyboard/E-DEVICE-DESIGN.md` — followed exactly.
**Origin credit (Panel 1 title card, and closing card):** designed by Aundre Wright, early 2026, through AI agents in the Muse app
**Recast (2026-11-23, Aundre's direct order — "Because I built the thing, I don't need no crew"):** the 2194 crew (LEAD BUILDER, TECH, OPERATOR) is removed. AUNDRE is the sole builder in all 22 panels — he runs every station and every step himself. The only other person on screen is the unsubscribed new arrival in Panels 21–22, who is the public, not crew.

## Standing rules for this sequence

- **Camera:** coverage on every beat — wide, tracking, close-up and insert shots of the same action, cut like a real crew. The camera walks with Aundre. Nothing static, no locked-off shots, no slow zoom on a still.
- **Dialogue only.** Aundre talks — to camera, to his build log/recorder, or thinking aloud on camera. There is no narrator voice-over anywhere in this sequence.
- **2194 register:** scientist/philosopher terms — state, boundary condition, coherence, registry, state-step. No frequency / hum / pulse framing in dialogue, captions or sound notes.
- **Honest ground:** Panel 2 carries the printed line on a clean-room wall placard, in frame: "Casimir effect: measured fact. Net work from the vacuum: our leap." The fiction stays named as fiction.
- **The device is a system.** No panel shows anyone holding a glowing box. The only worn object is the Focus Band (Panel 14), and it is a key, never a source.
- **People:** Aundre alone, photoreal, grounded workwear under a clean-room suit. He is the designer and the builder — every station, every step, his hands. One other person appears: the new arrival in Panels 21–22 (no credentials, no subscription — the public receiving the free shield).

**Panel count: 22 panels.**

---

### PANEL 1 — ENTERING THE CLEAN ROOM
- **Shot:** Wide tracking — camera walks backward ahead of Aundre through the airlock, then turns and follows him in.
- **Visual:** Airlock doors cycle; Aundre enters alone in a clean-room suit, passing under a plain sign: FABRICATION — LEVEL 1: TOOLS THAT MAKE TOOLS. Title card over the wide: THE BUILD.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to his build log): "Build log, day one. Everything in this room exists to make something finer than itself. I remember that, the rest is procedure."
- **Sound:** Airlock pressure sigh, suit fabric, soft air-handling room tone, boot covers on clean floor.

### PANEL 2 — THE HONEST GROUND
- **Shot:** Close-up tracking along a wall placard line, then tilt up to the fabrication floor beyond it.
- **Visual:** The placard in frame, engraved, unlit: "Casimir effect: measured fact. Net work from the vacuum: our leap." Beyond it, the floor: lithography bay, deposition chambers, polishing stations in a row.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to camera): "I printed the doubt on the wall myself. So nobody sells it as magic. It's engineering. Careful engineering."
- **Sound:** Room tone continues, distant chamber door chime, trolley wheels passing.

### PANEL 3 — ELECTRON-BEAM LITHOGRAPHY
- **Shot:** Insert close-up — the e-beam column and stage; cut to a tracking close-up over the pattern appearing on the wafer-scale blank.
- **Visual:** A beam writes the nanoscale pattern for a membrane frame: lines finer than the eye can judge, shown on the inspection monitor as a magnified grid. Aundre watches the write counter climb.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (thinking aloud): "The beam draws smaller than light can see. That's the point. The Room is built at the size the vacuum notices."
- **Sound:** Vacuum pumps low and steady, stage servos in small precise steps, keyboard taps.

### PANEL 4 — ATOMIC-LAYER DEPOSITION
- **Shot:** Slow tracking dolly along the deposition chamber window; insert of the layer counter.
- **Visual:** Inside the chamber, a plate blank gains its gold coat one atomic layer at a time. The counter ticks: LAYER 1… 2… 3… A side graphic in the glass reflection shows a single atom row being laid.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to camera): "One atom thick. Per pass. Atom by atom. That's not a metaphor here. That's the recipe."
- **Sound:** Chamber valve clicks in a steady cycle, gas line ticks, the counter's soft electronic blip per layer.

### PANEL 5 — ION-BEAM POLISHING
- **Shot:** Close-up on the polishing station; camera drifts with the plate carrier as it rotates under the ion column.
- **Visual:** A finished membrane plate turns slowly; its face is matte-flat, precision-flat. Aundre lifts it by the frame edge only, gloved, and sets it in a carrier of identical plates.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (thinking aloud): "Roughness is noise. Noise drowns the Room. So I polish until the surface stops arguing."
- **Sound:** Ion source tick and hiss, carrier latch clicks, glove leather creak.

### PANEL 6 — MEASURING WITH LIGHT
- **Shot:** Insert — interferometer screen; then a tracking shot as Aundre carries the measured plate toward the stack bench.
- **Visual:** Interferometric fringes settle into straight, even bands on screen: the plate measures flat. A readout gives the surface figure in nanometres.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to his build log): "Can't set a nanometre gap with a ruler. You measure with light itself, or you don't measure at all."
- **Sound:** Screen fan, soft confirmation tone, footsteps on clean floor, carrier handle clicks.

### PANEL 7 — THE CAVITY STACK
- **Shot:** Wide at the stack bench, pushing in to close-up as plates are layered.
- **Visual:** Aundre layers membrane frames one by one into the niobium frame — twenty-four layers — while corner piezoelectric actuators are fitted and torqued. The stack grows from parts to a single object: THE ROOM.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (thinking aloud): "Every gap the same. Every gap a decision. Spacing sets the state. Set it wrong, it's furniture. Set it right, it's a room the vacuum has to respect."
- **Sound:** Torque driver in short bursts, frame bolts seating, actuator test steps — tiny mechanical clicks.

### PANEL 8 — THE REGISTRY RING
- **Shot:** Tracking circle shot around the sensor ring as modules are seated.
- **Visual:** Twelve shielded sensor cans placed one by one into the cryogenic ring by Aundre's hands; braided copper thermal straps dressed and clamped; fibre loom routed to the single service edge. The centre void stays empty, waiting.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to camera): "SQUID-class. Single-photon. Reads fast, touches almost nothing. And if it can't read it, I can't spend it. No registry, no device. State is the address."
- **Sound:** Module seat clicks, strap clamp snaps, fibre connectors clicking home in sequence.

### PANEL 9 — THE TRANSDUCER BASE
- **Shot:** Low wide tracking under the crane as the lattice base is moved; insert on a single loop cell.
- **Visual:** The 8 × 8 superconducting loop lattice on its plinth is lowered into the assembly frame; piezoelectric blocks and copper bus bars are connected at the service edge. A coin beside one cell gives the scale, then Aundre's hand removes the coin before close-out.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (thinking aloud): "The muscle goes at the bottom. Everything the Room earns, this catches. Metered in state-steps. Not gallons. Steps."
- **Sound:** Crane motor, rigging straps under load, plinth bolts, bus bar clamp torque.

### PANEL 10 — THE GOVERNOR IS A SHAPE
- **Shot:** Close-up inspection — camera travels along the asymmetric frame profile with a gauge in Aundre's hand; insert on the interrupted coupling route.
- **Visual:** Aundre inspects a second frame section and rejects it: its coupling route runs continuous. The accepted frame shows the drawn gap — no material, no path — where a backward channel would have to pass. He stamps the accepted frame himself.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to his build log): "This one's wrong. The path runs through. It doesn't ship. The locks aren't settings. They're the shape. You can't patch out a shape."
- **Sound:** Gauge sliding on metal, stamp thud, rejected frame set down hard on the scrap bench.

### PANEL 11 — THE KEEPER: COLD
- **Shot:** Wide tracking following the refrigerator column as Aundre rolls it to the assembly; tilt up its full height.
- **Visual:** The dilution-refrigerator column docks beside the cold core; stage plates descend in order inside the open column; lines are connected and leak-checked, all by Aundre.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to camera): "How cold? Nearly as cold as the universe allows. Heat is decoherence. I'm buying quiet, at the lowest temperature there is."
- **Sound:** Column casters, line couplers, leak-check spray hiss, Aundre's own checklist read aloud to the empty room in the background.

### PANEL 12 — THE KEEPER: THE MESH
- **Shot:** Tracking along the compute rack bank as modules slide in under Aundre's hands; close-up on his hands seating the last module.
- **Visual:** Radiation-hard compute modules fill the racks — ordinary-looking hardware, deliberately. The Faraday shell panel, real woven metal mesh, is lifted into place over the bank by Aundre.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (thinking aloud): "Looks like any data centre. It should. The extraordinary part isn't the boxes. It's what they hold — a state, held still, paid for every second."
- **Sound:** Rack rails sliding, module latches, mesh panel frame bolts, rack fans rising then settling.

### PANEL 13 — TOOLS BUILDING TOOLS
- **Shot:** Wide two-level shot — camera rises on a platform lift from the older assembly line to the new one, Aundre riding it.
- **Visual:** On the lower level, an earlier-generation assembly rig — visibly cruder — holds a workpiece steady while a finer rig works on it. On the upper level, the new rig assembles parts finer than its own. A ladder of generations in one frame, Aundre standing between the levels.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to camera): "The old machine is building the new machine. And the Field holds the state steady while it works. The ladder builds itself, rung by rung. That's the whole history of how I got here."
- **Sound:** Two generations of machinery at once — coarse servos below, fine servos above — lift platform motor.

### PANEL 14 — THE FOCUS BAND
- **Shot:** Close-up tracking across a worktable: fabric loom, node press, finished band lifted by Aundre.
- **Visual:** Conductive fabric comes off a small loom, silver thread grid visible; ceramic temple nodes are pressed in; Aundre fits the finished Focus Band on himself, adjusts it once, and reads the inside label: A KEY, NOT A SOURCE.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to camera): "It doesn't hold any power. None. It's a key. Keys don't power the house. They just fit the lock — after the safety exam says your hand is steady."
- **Sound:** Loom shuttle, node press thump, fabric and strap sounds, a quiet room beyond the table.

### PANEL 15 — ASSEMBLY IN VACUUM
- **Shot:** Wide through the vacuum-chamber viewport, then camera moves inside on the chamber's internal rail for a close pass.
- **Visual:** The chamber is pumped down — a gauge falls through decades of pressure. Inside, manipulators Aundre set and runs lower the Cavity stack into the Registry ring's centre void. Air is gone; the device is being born in a vacuum better than deep space.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to his build log): "Chamber's below orbital pressure. Air is noise. It's born in quiet or it isn't born at all."
- **Sound:** Pump note falling away to near-silence, manipulator joints, the soft clunk of the stack seating — muffled by vacuum.

### PANEL 16 — CLOSING THE SYSTEM
- **Shot:** Tracking shot following the Faraday shell door as Aundre walks it closed alone, its full length.
- **Visual:** Transducer base below, Cavity centred, Registry around it, Keeper cold and mesh around all of it. The woven shell door swings shut and is dogged down, bolt by bolt, Aundre walking its length himself.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to his build log, counting it back to himself): "Count it back. Room. Registry. Keeper. Transducer. Governor in the geometry. And the coupler stays outside. The coupler stays outside."
- **Sound:** Door rollers, dogging bolts in sequence, suit movement, room tone thinning as the seal seats.

### PANEL 17 — COOLDOWN
- **Shot:** Insert chain — temperature readouts descending stage by stage, intercut with a tracking close-up of frost forming on external line insulation.
- **Visual:** Stage readouts step down in order; the cold core's number falls and keeps falling. Aundre watches in reflection in the console glass — his face, not a light show.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (thinking aloud): "Still falling. It falls until the noise gives up. Then I hold."
- **Sound:** Cryo lines ticking as they contract, readout tones spaced wide apart, held breath quiet in the room.

### PANEL 18 — FIRST HOLD
- **Shot:** Close two-shot of Aundre at the console, camera drifting slowly across his face; insert of the Registry readout settling.
- **Visual:** The Registry readout, jittering at first, settles into a steady address. The Keeper's compute load climbs and levels. The state holds — a full second, then five, then ten, counted on the console clock.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to his build log): "Registry has it. State is read. It's holding. Coherence, bought and paid for. Don't touch anything."
- **Sound:** Room tone at its quietest, a single steady console tone, the clock's soft count.

### PANEL 19 — FIRST STATE-STEP
- **Shot:** Close-up on the boundary-condition control as Aundre's hand moves it one detent; cut to the stack section monitor.
- **Visual:** On Aundre's steady move, the held state is allowed to step down exactly one step. On the monitor the state line descends a single clean stair. The Governor ceiling gauge sits unmoved, well inside its stop.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (thinking aloud): "One step. Carefully. Let it change its mind by exactly one step. Stepping… now. Clean step. Registry confirms."
- **Sound:** Control detent click, a beat of silence, one low mechanical note from the lattice base as it takes the step.

### PANEL 20 — THE HARVEST
- **Shot:** Tracking shot from the Transducer bus bars along the cable run to the load bench.
- **Visual:** The caught step arrives at the load bench as electrical work: a workshop motor turns, a work lamp lights — ordinary loads, honestly drawn, powered by one state-step. The meter on the bench increments by one: STATE-STEPS HARVESTED: 1.
- **Dialogue:** AUNDRE (to camera): "That's it. A lamp and a motor. First E, candle-sized. Every century of power started exactly this small."
- **Sound:** Motor turning over and running, lamp switch click, the meter's single increment clack, Aundre's exhale and quiet laugh.

### PANEL 21 — THE SHIELD IS FREE
- **Shot:** Wide tracking — a second operator position outside the shell is brought online by Aundre, without any subscription terminal in frame.
- **Visual:** A new arrival — no credentials, no payment point anywhere in the shot — is fitted with a band by Aundre and the shield layer comes up around the training position. The Governor diagram on the wall shows the shield ring outside every tier.
- **Dialogue:** NEW ARRIVAL: "I don't have an account here." AUNDRE: "The shield isn't a tier. Peace is not a tier. That rule was cut into the geometry before any of us were born — 2026. It doesn't come out."
- **Sound:** Training room tone, band fitting sounds, shield system confirmation tone, distant fabrication floor.

### PANEL 22 — THE EXPLAINER (CLOSING PANEL)
- **Shot:** Tracking close shot — camera walks with Aundre out of the fabrication floor toward the finished, closed system; he stops, turns, and delivers the line to camera, in conversation, not narration. (Per the bible: "Aundre to camera.") The new arrival from Panel 21 stands with him, listening — she asks the question, as the public would.
- **Visual:** The closed system behind Aundre — quiet, ordinary-looking, enormous. The new arrival stands beside him; her reflection crosses the Faraday mesh as she settles. Closing card after the line: THE E DEVICE — designed by Aundre Wright, early 2026, through AI agents in the Muse app.
- **Dialogue:** NEW ARRIVAL: "So tell it plain. Where does the power come from?" AUNDRE (to camera): "We don't burn anything. We let the vacuum change its mind, carefully, and we catch it."
- **Sound:** Footsteps slowing to a stop, room tone settling, one distant chamber chime — then quiet under the closing card.
