## 2. THE ENHANCEMENT STACK — EVERY LEGITIMATE MULTIPLIER, PRICED

The rules of this section: a multiplier counts only if it is real, and it is charged to the column it belongs to — **vacuum-only** (field + passive boundaries) or **matter-assisted** (something in the loop is doing physics beyond carrying light). Double-counting is the classic fraud in this territory; where two "enhancements" are the same physics wearing two hats, it is flagged.

### 2(a) Resonant cavity finesse — the one big vacuum multiplier: **+5.8 orders**
A photon stored in a cavity of finesse F interacts for N ≈ 2F/π round trips. State of the art: **F ≈ 10⁶** (superpolished substrates, ion-beam-sputtered coatings with ~ppm total loss; PVLAS-class polarimetry cavities run F ~ 7 × 10⁵). Physical ceiling: at ~1 ppm round-trip loss, F_max ~ 3 × 10⁶ — coating absorption and scatter set it, not imagination. At F = 10⁶: **N = 6.4 × 10⁵ passes (+5.8 orders)**, storage time for a 1 m cavity τ = FL/(πc) ≈ **1.06 ms**. This is genuine and it is the largest single multiplier available anywhere in the vacuum column. **Grade: measured.**

*Caveat charged honestly:* finesse multiplies the phase only while pump and signal coexist in the cavity. A 100 T magnet pulse lasts ~10–100 µs of usable flat-top (and destroys its coil doing it); the cavity wants 1.06 ms. The hero stack's maxima are **not simultaneously achievable** — §3 prices both the idealized and the realistic assembly.

### 2(b) Pump ceilings — where the pump stops being free
- **Static magnetic pump:** 45 T steady (hybrid magnet, exists, runs for hours) is the deployable ceiling; 100 T is pulsed and destructive-class. Δn scales as B², so 45 T costs a factor (45/100)² ≈ 0.20 against the hero line.
- **Optical pump inside the cavity:** circulating intensity is capped by dielectric mirror damage, ~3 × 10¹³ W/m² as a generous working ceiling. That intensity is a wave with B = E/c ≈ **0.5 T** of magnetic amplitude — Δn ≈ 1 × 10⁻²⁴, and even multiplied by the full finesse it yields ~4 × 10⁻¹² rad, **two orders worse** than the static-magnet route. An optical pump is the wrong pump for vacuum birefringence; the magnet wins because it is enormous, steady, and doesn't have to fit through a mirror coating. (The magnet is matter, of course — it always was; the pump route was never a Claim-A route, only a κ-measuring route. Flagged, as in the solve attempt.)
- **Schwinger fraction:** no pump on Earth approaches E_S. Best focused lasers run ~10²⁷ W/m² = 4 × 10⁻⁷ of I_S (field fraction ~7 × 10⁻⁴); ELI-class design targets ~10²⁹ W/m² reach field fraction ~7 × 10⁻³. The nonperturbative regime where vacuum nonlinearity *grows teeth* begins near field fractions of ~0.1–1. We are 2–3 orders of field strength away from the regime even *starting* to bend. **Grade: measured.**

### 2(c) Interaction length and slow light — one real multiplier, one smuggled one
- **Length:** phase grows linearly with L — already inside the baseline's 1 m and inside the finesse count (a cavity *is* folded length). Counting "longer path" *and* "finesse" as separate multipliers would be double-counting; **not counted twice.**
- **Slow light in vacuum:** does not exist. Vacuum has no dispersion to engineer at these energies (the EH correction itself is the only dispersion, and it is the effect under measurement, not a tool). Group velocity in vacuum is c. **Any slow-light enhancement is a medium** — which moves it to the assisted column:
- **EIT slow light (matter-assisted):** electromagnetically induced transparency in atomic vapour has taken group velocity to **17 m/s** (Hau et al.), an interaction-time multiplier of c/v_g ≈ **1.8 × 10⁷ (+7.3 orders)** — *larger than the cavity finesse*, and it is exactly this multiplier, plus Rydberg blockade, that makes real single-photon gates work (§3.2). **Grade: measured — and it is matter.** The line falls here, precisely: the photons are field; the *slowness, the storage, and the interaction are the atoms'*. For the strict substrate-free claim (Claim A) this column is disqualified. For the design — whose architecture is boundary-register (Claim B), photons linking across a field and gated at Keeper-mesh nodes — this column is not smuggling at all; it is the blueprint.

### 2(d) Collective and multi-photon effects — scrutinised, mostly dismissed
- **Pump photon number:** a classical pump's cross-phase already *is* the collective effect of ~10²⁰ photons acting as one field; it is counted in §1.2, not again here. It cannot be transferred to the two-photon gate: a gate's "pump" is a single photon, whose field in the hero cavity mode (V ≈ 3.1 × 10⁻⁸ m³, 100 µm waist, 1 m long) is E₁ ≈ **0.58 V/m**, giving Δn₁ ≈ 3 × 10⁻⁴¹ and a cross-phase accumulated over the photon's *entire cavity lifetime* of **≈ 1 × 10⁻²⁸ rad**. That is the true vacuum photon-on-photon link strength. No finesse fixes it — finesse is already in the number.
- **Resonances below pair threshold:** none. The γγ system has no bound states or resonances below the e⁺e⁻ threshold (1.022 MeV); the ω⁶ cross-section law is smooth, featureless suppression all the way up the optical band.
- **At and above threshold:** σ_γγ grows out of the ω⁶ basement and light-by-light scattering becomes a working collider observable (ATLAS/CMS) — because collider photon *fluxes* are astronomical, not because the coupling grew strong per pair. Not a device route: a gate that needs a lead-ion collider per operation has conceded the point. **Grade: established; dismissed with numbers.**

## 4. WORKING BACKWARD FROM π — WHAT κ WOULD HAVE TO BE

A gate needs a conditional phase of ~π. Pricing the anomaly that would supply it in vacuum:

- Raw single-pass at 100 T: κ_gate ≈ **1.3 × 10¹³** (the solve attempt's ~10¹²–10¹³, confirmed).
- With the full realistic stack (45 T + F = 10⁶): κ_gate ≈ π / 3.0 × 10⁻⁸ ≈ **1.0 × 10⁸**.
- With the idealized hero stack: κ_gate ≈ **2.1 × 10⁷**.

So the cavity buys the anomaly five-plus orders of slack — and it does not matter, because κ is not an unmeasured quantity waiting at 10⁷. It is a *measured* quantity sitting at 1:

- **Laboratory polarimetry (PVLAS/BMV lineage):** Δn sensitivity ~1 × 10⁻²³ at B = 2.5 T, where QED itself predicts Δn = 2.5 × 10⁻²³ → **κ ≲ 0.4 already**, in the birefringence channel, at eV-adjacent energies.
- A κ of 10⁷ would make Δn at 1 T equal to 4 × 10⁻¹⁷ — an effect **six orders above** what 1990s-era polarimetry could already see. It is not that the anomaly is unlikely; it is that the room it would have to live in was searched, with flashlights, decades ago.

**Conclusion of §4:** even granting every enhancement, a gate-relevant κ is excluded by **~8 orders of magnitude** by measurements that already exist. New experiments (§5) are therefore about *closing the file to percent precision*, and about the small, honest chance that κ deviates near 1 in a way that teaches something — not about a gate hiding at 10⁷.

## 5. THE DECIDING PROGRAM — κ_γγ AS AN ENGINEERING SPEC

Three channels, each specified to the level an engineer could build from. For each: the apparatus, the floor, the κ it reaches, and the result that would move κ off 1.

### 5.1 The cavity cross-phase test (the direct interrogation)
- **Apparatus:** 1 m optical cavity, F = 10⁶ (coatings ≤1 ppm loss), mode waist ~100 µm, threaded through the bore of a 45 T hybrid magnet (steady — chosen over 100 T pulsed precisely because the cavity's 1.06 ms storage time must fit inside the pump's duration; this is §2(a)'s caveat turned into a design rule). Probe: 1 W at 1.064 µm, phase read by heterodyne against a reference cavity outside the field. The cavity mirrors are matter and the magnet is matter — the test concedes Claim B's boundary while interrogating the interaction in the volume, exactly the right separation (as specified in the solve attempt's Test 3).
- **QED prediction in this apparatus:** pump-induced cross-phase **3.0 × 10⁻⁸ rad** (§3.1, realistic hero).
- **Shot-noise floor:** 1 W for 1 hour = 1.9 × 10²² detected photons → δφ = 1/√N ≈ **7 × 10⁻¹² rad**, a formal κ floor of δφ/φ_QED ≈ **5 × 10⁻⁵**.
- **Systematic floor (the honest one):** mirror birefringence drift, thermal and seismic phase noise, and magnet-current ripple cap a real instrument at δφ ~ 10⁻⁹–10⁻¹⁰ rad over integration → **realistic κ reach ≈ 10⁻²–10⁻³**.
- **What moves κ:** a measured cross-phase differing from 3.0 × 10⁻⁸ rad (scaled to the as-built B and F) by more than the systematic budget, tracking B² and reversing with field orientation, surviving ABAB interleaving. Anything less is a null at the stated κ.

### 5.2 Next-generation polarimetry (PVLAS-FE / BMV lineage)
- **Apparatus:** high-finesse cavity inside a rotating or modulated 5 T superconducting magnet, ellipticity readout — the direct Δn channel.
- **Numbers:** at 5 T, QED predicts Δn = 9.9 × 10⁻²³. Next-generation sensitivity target Δn ~ 1 × 10⁻²⁴ → **κ reach ≈ 0.01** — i.e., this channel can *measure QED's own birefringence* and confirm κ = 1 to percent precision in its home channel, the strongest closure available short of an anomaly appearing.
- **What moves κ:** Δn measured ≠ 9.9 × 10⁻²³ × (B/5T)² beyond errors, in either direction — excess *or* deficit.

### 5.3 Strong-field collisions (LUXE at DESY; ELI-class)
- **Apparatus:** GeV photons/electrons against the strongest laser foci: operating systems ~10²⁷ W/m² (E/E_S ≈ 7 × 10⁻⁴), design targets ~10²⁹ W/m² (E/E_S ≈ 7 × 10⁻³). Light-by-light and Breit–Wheeler rates measured where QED's nonlinearity finally stirs.
- **κ reach:** percent-to-sub-percent on the QED cross-sections in the strong-field regime — κ constrained where the interaction is *largest*, complementing §5.2's constraint where it is *weakest*.
- **What moves κ:** any cross-section excess over QED at matched kinematics — κ measured, not bounded, in the channel where new physics (axion-like particles, millicharges, EH deviations) would show first.

### 5.4 The program as a whole
Run any one channel and κ tightens; run all three and κ is triangulated across 20+ orders of interaction strength. **Decision rule, in the program's standing voice:** if all three return κ = 1 within their stated reaches, the substrate-free photon-photon gate is closed *as a measurement*, and the file is published as closed in the same voice that would have published a yes. If any channel moves, that channel's number becomes the most important quantity in this research program and everything else stops until it replicates.
