## 8. THE TWO DOORS
![The Two Doors](media-generation-h-two-doors-v2-0-a36786b2-97f3-49a4-8b6f-6f63b261b3f7.webp)

- **What you're looking at:** the device blueprint with the research drawn ON it — two numbered test points marked on the machine itself. Marker 1 sits at the Coupler dock, where intention meets the boundary. Marker 2 sits beside an inset of a high-finesse optical cavity — two curved mirrors, two photon paths crossing — at the Registry/Emitter side. The bottom strip carries the verdict: ONE DOOR CLOSED BY THE NUMBERS. ONE DOOR OPEN — AND IT IS OURS.
- **Door One — λ_χ, the Wright coupling (STATUS: OPEN):** the question of whether the boundary itself couples to the held state beyond standard physics. Its deciding experiment is the entropy-control test, in one line: same drive, two registers — structured vs scrambled; the effect must track the state, not the dial. If it tracks the dial, it's an artifact and the door closes. If it tracks the state, physics has a new term and the design has its mechanism.
- **Door Two — κ_γγ, photon on photon (STATUS: CLOSED AS A ROUTE):** the question of whether light alone can gate light in empty space. The numbers, re-derived: σ_γγ(1 eV) ≈ 7 × 10⁻⁷⁰ m²; the full honest enhancement stack (million-finesse cavity, strongest magnets) closes 5.8 of the 13.1 orders to a classical pump phase — and the true photon-on-photon channel still sits ~28 orders short per pair. Existing polarimetry already bounds κ ≲ 0.4; a working gate would need κ ≳ 2 × 10⁷ — excluded by about 8 orders. The specified cavity test (1 m, finesse 10⁶, 45 T, heterodyne readout) can push the bound toward 10⁻²–10⁻³: a closure program that can answer NO in ink. Not solved — ruled out.
- **What it does:** it turns "we don't know" into a drawing with two addresses on it. Anybody reading this blueprint can see exactly where the unknowns live on the machine, what experiment decides each one, and what the current score is.
- **How it helps everyday life:** the boundary-assisted result is the everyday part — photon links gated by material nodes at the boundary are measured physics (0.1–π rad per pair), which is exactly how this design says its agents coordinate: the field carries, the matter decides. The help on sheets 5–7 stands on the road that stayed open.
- **Honest ground:** MEASURED (the QED baseline, the polarimetry bounds, the boundary-assisted gate phases) · CLOSED AS A ROUTE (κ_γγ — excluded by ~8 orders, not a matter of opinion) · OPEN (λ_χ — prior expectation probably zero, one experiment decides).
