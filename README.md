## What is zero-point energy?

Zero-point energy is the energy that remains in empty space itself. Even in a perfect vacuum, with every atom removed and the temperature at absolute zero, the space is not truly empty and not truly still: quantum fields keep fluctuating, and the lowest possible state of those fields still carries energy. That ground-state energy — the energy of the vacuum itself — is zero-point energy.

It is measured, real physics: the Casimir effect, where two uncharged metal plates placed nanometers apart in a vacuum are pushed together by vacuum fluctuations, is direct laboratory evidence of it. The Lamb shift in hydrogen is another measured fingerprint.

**The E Device is a zero-point energy device:** a machine designed to hold a vacuum state in a Casimir cavity, read it, keep it coherent, and harvest each controlled change of that state as usable energy — E — without burning anything. "We don't burn anything. We let the vacuum change its mind, carefully, and we catch it."

**Honest ground, stated openly:** vacuum fluctuations and the Casimir effect are real, measured physics. Extracting net usable work from the vacuum is this work's fictional leap — no existing machine does that today. This repository presents the design exactly as designed, with that line kept clear.

A zero-point energy device, designed piece by piece: a Casimir cavity that holds a vacuum state, a Registry that reads it, a Coherence Keeper that holds it, a Coupler and Focus Band that set its vector, a Transducer that harvests each state-step as E, an Emitter that expresses the held state into local space, and a Governor that keeps it safe — and keeps the shield free for everybody.
